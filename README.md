🏗️ SiteSync Next-Gen: Construction Waste & Surplus Material Matcher
A ConTech platform for real-time optimization, removal, and matching of surplus construction earthworks, sand, crushed concrete, and clean waste.

💡 Use Case & Core Problem
The system solves a classic challenge in construction site logistics: Site A pays a fortune for the disposal of surplus soil/concrete, while nearby Site B pays a fortune to purchase aggregate fill material.

SiteSync connects the two in real time, eliminating unnecessary transport, saving tens of thousands in dumping fees and fuel, and reducing carbon emissions.
